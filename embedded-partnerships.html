Frequently asked questions

Before we get in the room.

Every final scope is documented in a service agreement and statement of work.

Is this the same as hiring a marketing agency?

Not exactly. The model may include agency-style execution, but it is designed around cross-functional coordination with leadership and internal staff across brand, content, partnerships, experiences, and business development.

Does Mogul replace our internal sales or marketing team?

No. We work best alongside internal expertise, taking ownership of agreed responsibilities and strengthening the connection between teams, priorities, and execution.

Are specific leads, revenue, partners, or creators guaranteed?

No. Mogul can develop the strategy, materials, targets, outreach, and management process, but cannot guarantee participation by a specific party or a particular commercial result.

What is included each month?

That depends on the approved scope. Locations, platforms, production days, content volume, meetings, reporting, revision limits, travel, paid media, vendors, rush work, and exclusions are agreed before work begins.

How long is an embedded engagement?

The recommended term depends on the size of the opportunity and work required. The service agreement defines the initial term, renewal process, payment schedule, and termination rights.

What happens after we submit the consultation form?

Mogul reviews the information for fit and follows up using the contact details provided. Submitting the form does not create a client relationship or reserve capacity.
