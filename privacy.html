Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information Mogul Luxury Brand Management (“MLBM,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect through this website, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Effective: September 17, 2026

InformationHow we use itSharingRetentionYour choicesSecurityContact
Plain-language summaryWe use information you choose to provide to respond to you and discuss potential services. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you contact us, request information, or become a client, we may collect your name, email address, business or brand information, project details, communications, billing details, and any other information you choose to provide.

Website and device information

Our hosting and security providers may automatically process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, timestamps, and security or diagnostic logs. This information supports website delivery, reliability, and fraud prevention.

Inquiry form

When you submit the website inquiry form, the information you enter is sent to a Google Sheet managed by MLBM using Google Apps Script, and a copy is emailed to MLBM. Google processes this information as our service provider under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not send an automated confirmation email to the address you enter. Do not include sensitive financial, government identification, health, password, or account information in the form.

2. How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to inquiries and communicate with you;
  • evaluate, provide, and manage requested services;
  • prepare proposals, invoices, and client records;
  • maintain, secure, and improve our website and operations;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce agreements; and
  • protect the rights, safety, and property of MLBM, our clients, and others.

3. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to service providers, including Google (Google Workspace, Sheets, and Apps Script), that support hosting, communications, form delivery, payments, professional advice, and business operations; to a successor in connection with a merger, sale, or reorganization; or when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights and safety. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Cookies and external services

This site does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Basic hosting technologies and external font delivery may process technical information needed to load and secure the site. If analytics, advertising, or additional tracking technologies are introduced, this notice will be updated and any required choices will be provided.

5. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, including client service, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, security, and legal compliance. Retention periods vary according to the type of information and our obligations. Inquiry form submissions are stored in MLBM’s Google Sheet and email account until they are no longer needed for these purposes.

6. Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information you have provided to us. We will review requests and respond as required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information when legally permitted or required.

7. Security and children

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. This website and our services are intended for adults and business clients, not children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Third-party links and changes

This website may link to third-party services that have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for those services. We may update this notice from time to time and will post the revised effective date on this page.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Mogul Luxury Brand Management through the contact channel listed on our website. Please do not include sensitive financial, government identification, health, or account information in a general inquiry.

Attorney review recommendedThis notice is a practical website draft and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed against MLBM’s actual tools, vendors, data practices, and service locations before the website is opened to the public.
