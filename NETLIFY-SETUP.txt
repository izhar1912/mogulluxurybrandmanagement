MLBM — SETUP GUIDE
==================

The inquiry form saves every submission to a Google Sheet and emails it to
melody@mlbmrep.com. Visitors only see the Thank You page; they receive no email.

Do Part A once, then Part B (deploy). Takes about 10 minutes.


PART A — CONNECT THE GOOGLE SHEET (one time)
--------------------------------------------
Sign in to the Google account that should own the sheet (ideally Melody's).

1. Go to https://sheets.new and name the sheet "MLBM Inquiries".
2. In the sheet: Extensions > Apps Script.
3. Delete everything in Code.gs, paste in the contents of mlbm-form-backend.gs,
   and click Save.
4. In the function dropdown at the top choose "setup", then click Run.
   Google will ask for permission: Review permissions > choose the account >
   Advanced > Go to project (unsafe) > Allow.
   (This is your own script asking to edit this sheet and send email.)
   An "Inquiries" tab with column headers now appears in the sheet.
5. Optional: choose "sendTestEmail" and click Run to confirm Melody receives it.
6. Click Deploy > New deployment. Click the gear > Web app.
     Description:     MLBM form
     Execute as:      Me
     Who has access:  Anyone
   Click Deploy and copy the Web app URL (ends in /exec).
7. Open index.html in a text editor, find:
     PASTE_YOUR_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_WEB_APP_URL_HERE
   and replace it with the URL you copied. Save.


PART B — DEPLOY TO NETLIFY
--------------------------
1. Zip the site folder again (index.html must be at the top level of the ZIP)
   or drag the folder itself onto https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Open the live site, submit the form once, and confirm:
     - you land on the Thank You page
     - a new row appears in the Inquiries tab
     - melody@mlbmrep.com receives the email (reply goes straight to the visitor)


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- If you edit the Apps Script later: Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon >
  Version: New version > Deploy. This keeps the same URL.
  (Creating a brand-new deployment gives a new URL, which must go into index.html.)
- Google's daily email limit is about 100 for a free Gmail account and
  1,500 for Google Workspace. Rows are still saved if the limit is reached.
- The emails come from the Google account that owns the script.
- A hidden spam trap discards most bot submissions automatically.
- 404.html is picked up by Netlify automatically for any missing page.
- Styles and scripts are versioned (?v=3). Netlify caches /assets/ for a year,
  so bump the number in every .html file whenever styles.css or site.js changes.

Pages: Home, Capabilities, Embedded Brand Growth Partnerships, Roster,
Privacy Notice, Service Terms, Thank You, 404.
